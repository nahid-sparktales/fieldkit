# Returns and refunds

Trail Supply is a fictional store in a local sandbox. Unused backpacks and bottles can be returned within 30 days of delivery with their original packaging. Start a return through support and include your order number. Items must be inspected before a real refund would be approved. Original express shipping charges are not refundable. Approved refunds go to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. For this sandbox, staff can approve simulated full or partial refunds; no money moves.
