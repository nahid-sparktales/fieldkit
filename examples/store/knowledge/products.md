# Products and care

The Summit Daypack costs $89, holds 24 liters, and has a padded laptop sleeve. Its fabric is water-resistant, not waterproof. Spot-clean it with mild soap and air-dry; do not put it in a washing machine. The insulated Trail Bottle costs $29 and holds 750 ml. Wash the bottle and lid by hand. Neither product is intended for use with a dishwasher. Prices are fictional US dollars.
